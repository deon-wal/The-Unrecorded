Institutional Framework · CANON

The Office of Reconciliation

Established by the Confirmed Events Act after the Calder grain-store fire, which courts, insurers and newspapers had dated differently. The first Register (March 1965) had 41 entries. Its serials ran to 44, so 3 were empty. Founding clerk Edith Marr's minute calls the gaps "reserved", records that two proofing clerks disagreed on the entry count, and says the third later could not remember the disagreement.

The Flood Barrier

North of Amble on the Northumberland coast; a flood barrier nearly a mile long; every household has paid a £38 annual levy for 43 years "following the events of 2 June." Maintained by Colm Aird for 31 years.
